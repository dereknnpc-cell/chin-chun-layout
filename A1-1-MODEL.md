# A1-1 photo-reference model — visual draft

The supplied photographs show the full A1-1 EVA process from several sides.
The right end is the entrance: material comes in on the green conveyor, passes
through the processing rollers beside the large drum, then the sheet runs
diagonally left across the open frame to the silver and brown collection rolls.
The drum sits toward the right end, with the long open transfer span and a red
control cabinet extending to its left. A second cabinet is beside the drum.
The 2.5D model depicts this sequence, the support frame, guide rollers, and
overhead fan. Original photos and people are not embedded in the application.

The model uses the existing A1-1 CAD footprint (initially 13.6 × 3.7 m), x/y,
rotation, and resized planar dimensions. It does not change the 2D drawing,
cloud records, or machine data. Vertical dimensions and component proportions
remain visual estimates from the photos, not survey data. The owner confirmed
that the green conveyor is to the right of the large wheel and that this is the
entrance end. Component spacing and the left collection end follow the new
multi-angle photos. Both ends are labeled in the close-up to make the full
material path legible. The H1-1 model remains independent and unchanged.
