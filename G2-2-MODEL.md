# G2-2 photo-reference model

Based on five owner-supplied photos: both ends, side, and elevated roller-bed views.
Retains the existing 4.0 × 2.4 m CAD footprint and placement. Geometry follows CAD
rotation and resizing, without changing the layout records.

Included: cream side housings, orange recessed deck, independent green/metal
rollers, bare winding shafts and support arms, pneumatic knife holders with blue
air lines, side controls, and thin overhead lighting frame.

Excluded: foam, material rolls, paper strips, loose cores, people, factory columns,
nearby stock, and furniture. Hidden internal mechanisms are not reconstructed.

Height approximately 2.3 m and component proportions are photographic estimates.
Local machine axes run across the short footprint dimension. Feed/winding
direction relative to the floor plan is unconfirmed; no flow arrows are asserted.
This is display geometry, not a surveyed or fabrication model.
